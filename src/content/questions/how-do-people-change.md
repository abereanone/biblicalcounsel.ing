People change as God, by His Spirit and Word, renews their hearts, and as they respond by turning from sin and toward Christ in specific, daily obedience. Change is God's work and our work together, and it lasts a lifetime.

## Proofs
- Philippians 2:12-13
- 2 Corinthians 3:18
- Ephesians 4:22-24
- Romans 12:2
- Titus 2:11-12

The Bible calls this change *sanctification*. It is different from justification, which is God's once-for-all declaration that we are righteous in Christ. Sanctification is the ongoing process of becoming in practice what we already are in Christ.

Two truths hold together: "continue to work out your salvation with fear and trembling. For it is God who works in you to will and to act on behalf of His good purpose" (Philippians 2:12-13). We work, because God is working.

How does it happen?

- **Beholding Christ.** "We... are being transformed into His image" as we look at His glory (2 Corinthians 3:18).
- **Grace teaching us.** The grace of God "instructs us to renounce ungodliness and worldly passions, and to live sensible, upright, and godly lives" (Titus 2:12).
- **Renewing the mind** by the Word (Romans 12:2).
- **Putting off and putting on** specific sins and virtues (Ephesians 4:22-24).
- **The means of grace**: Scripture, prayer, the Lord's Supper, and the fellowship of the church.

Change is usually slow. Expect progress, not perfection, and do not despise small steps.
