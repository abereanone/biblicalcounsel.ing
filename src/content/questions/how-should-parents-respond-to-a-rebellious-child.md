With prayer, patience, clear boundaries and unwavering love. Speak truth, let your child experience the consequences of his choices, refuse to enable sin, and keep the door open for his return, as the father did for the prodigal son.

## Proofs
- Luke 15:11-24
- Ephesians 6:4
- Proverbs 22:15
- 1 Corinthians 13:7
- James 5:16

Few sorrows are as deep as watching a child walk away from God. Many faithful parents have known it, and it does not necessarily mean they failed.

Jesus' parable of the prodigal son (Luke 15:11-24) shows a father who let his son go, did not chase him with money, and watched for his return. When the son came back, "while he was still in the distance, his father saw him and was filled with compassion. He ran to his son, embraced him, and kissed him" (Luke 15:20).

Practical guidance:

- **Pray without ceasing.** Only God changes hearts. "The prayer of a righteous man has great power to prevail" (James 5:16).
- **Examine yourself humbly.** Ask whether there is anything you need to confess to your child, and do it.
- **Keep clear boundaries.** For a child living at home, set reasonable rules and consequences. For an adult child, do not fund sin or rescue him from every consequence.
- **Keep loving.** Love "bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things" (1 Corinthians 13:7). Stay in contact where you can.
- **Get support.** Ask your church to pray with you, and seek counsel for your own heart.
- **Watch for the return**, and when it comes, welcome it.

If a child is in danger, or endangering others, involve the proper authorities.
