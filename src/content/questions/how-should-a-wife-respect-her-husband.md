By honoring him, supporting his leadership in the home as to the Lord, and speaking of him and to him with respect. Biblical submission is a free, trusting choice made out of reverence for Christ; it never requires her to sin or to submit to abuse.

## Proofs
- Ephesians 5:22-24
- Ephesians 5:33
- 1 Peter 3:1-6
- Titus 2:4-5
- Acts 5:29

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

"Wives, submit to your husbands as to the Lord" (Ephesians 5:22), and "the wife must respect her husband" (Ephesians 5:33).

What this means:

- **It is directed to the Lord.** A wife's submission is first an act of trust in Christ, not a statement of her husband's worthiness.
- **It is respect in word and attitude.** Not belittling him, mocking him or undermining him before others; encouraging him and speaking well of him.
- **It supports his leadership.** She gladly helps him lead the family toward God, and she brings her wisdom and concerns to him openly.
- **It can be a witness.** Even husbands who do not obey the Word may "be won over without words by the behavior of their wives" (1 Peter 3:1).

What it does not mean:

- **It does not make her inferior.** Husband and wife are "fellow heirs of the gracious gift of life" (1 Peter 3:7).
- **It never requires sin.** "We must obey God rather than men" (Acts 5:29).
- **It never requires her to accept abuse.** A wife who is being harmed should get safe and get help.
