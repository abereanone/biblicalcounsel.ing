Loneliness is a real sorrow in a fallen world, and God cares about it. He is present with His people, He promises never to leave them, and He has placed them in a family, the church, where they can both receive and give love.

## Proofs
- Psalm 25:16
- Psalm 68:6
- Hebrews 13:5
- Genesis 2:18
- Hebrews 10:24-25

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

God said, "It is not good for the man to be alone" (Genesis 2:18), before sin entered the world. We were made for fellowship with God and with one another. Loneliness is not weakness or failure. David prayed, "Turn to me and be gracious, for I am lonely and afflicted" (Psalm 25:16).

What helps:

- **Bring it to God honestly.** He has promised, "Never will I leave you, never will I forsake you" (Hebrews 13:5).
- **Receive His provision.** "God settles the lonely in families" (Psalm 68:6). The church is God's family for His people, and it is meant to be a real household.
- **Show up.** "Let us not neglect meeting together" (Hebrews 10:25). Attend regularly, join a smaller group, and come early and stay late.
- **Reach out to others.** Many people around you are lonely too. Invite someone for a meal, visit a shut-in, serve in a ministry. Loneliness often eases as we turn outward in love.
- **Be patient.** Deep friendships take time.

If loneliness has become despair, tell your pastor and get help.
