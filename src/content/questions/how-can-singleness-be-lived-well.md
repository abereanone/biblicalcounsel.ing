Singleness is not a lesser calling. Scripture calls it a gift that can free a believer for undivided devotion to the Lord. It is lived well through contentment, purity, deep friendships in the church and wholehearted service, whether for a season or for life.

## Proofs
- 1 Corinthians 7:7-8
- 1 Corinthians 7:32-35
- Matthew 19:12
- Isaiah 56:4-5
- Philippians 4:11-13

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

Jesus was single. So, as far as we know, was Paul, who wrote, "I wish that all men were as I am. But each man has his own gift from God" (1 Corinthians 7:7). He explained the advantage: "The unmarried man is concerned about the work of the Lord, how he can please the Lord" (1 Corinthians 7:32).

Single believers sometimes feel overlooked in churches built around families. But God promises those who hold fast to His covenant "a memorial and a name better than that of sons and daughters" (Isaiah 56:5).

Living singleness well:

- **Learn contentment** without denying real longings. "I have learned to be content regardless of my circumstances" (Philippians 4:11). If you desire marriage, it is right to pray for it and pursue it wisely.
- **Pursue purity.** God's design for sex within marriage is good, and He gives grace to obey it.
- **Build deep friendships** in the church. Singleness is not meant to be lonely, and the church is family.
- **Serve wholeheartedly.** Use your time and freedom for Christ and others.
- **Do not wait for marriage to begin your life.** Your identity is in Christ, not in your relationship status.

Churches can help by including single members fully in their life, hospitality and leadership.
