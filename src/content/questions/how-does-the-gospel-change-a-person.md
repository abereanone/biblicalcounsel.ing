The gospel forgives our guilt, gives us a new identity in Christ, breaks sin's power over us, and gives us new love for God. Christians change not in order to be accepted, but because they already are.

## Proofs
- Romans 6:6
- 2 Corinthians 5:17
- Titus 2:11-14
- 2 Corinthians 5:14-15
- 1 John 4:19

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

Much counseling, secular and religious, says in effect: *do better and you will be better.* The gospel says something different: *you have been made new in Christ; now live like it.*

- **Forgiven.** In Christ there is no condemnation (Romans 8:1). Guilt no longer has to drive a believer to hide, perform or despair.
- **New.** "If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation" (2 Corinthians 5:17).
- **Free.** "Our old self was crucified with Him so that the body of sin might be rendered powerless, that we should no longer be slaves to sin" (Romans 6:6).
- **Loved, and loving.** "We love because He first loved us" (1 John 4:19). Christ's love "compels us" to live for Him (2 Corinthians 5:14).
- **Taught.** The grace that saves us also "instructs us to renounce ungodliness" (Titus 2:12).

So biblical counseling never offers rules without grace. The counselee is pointed to Christ for forgiveness when he fails, for strength when he is weak, and for a reason to obey that is deeper than fear: love for the One who died for him.
