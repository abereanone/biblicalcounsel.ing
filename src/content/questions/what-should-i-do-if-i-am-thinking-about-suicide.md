Get help right now. In the United States call or text 988, or call 911, or go to the nearest emergency room. Tell someone near you today. Your life is precious to God, this darkness can lift, and you do not have to face it alone.

## Proofs
- Psalm 34:18
- 1 Kings 19:4-8
- Psalm 139:7-10
- Deuteronomy 30:19
- Romans 8:38-39

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

**If you are in danger now, stop reading and call or text 988 (the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline), call 911, or go to an emergency room.** If you are outside the United States, call your local emergency number.

Then, today:

- **Tell someone.** A pastor, a family member, a friend. Say the words: "I have been thinking about ending my life." People who love you would far rather know.
- **Put distance between you and the means.** Give medications, weapons or anything you have thought of using to someone else to keep.
- **Do not be alone** tonight if you can help it.
- **See a doctor** soon. Physical illness, medications and exhaustion can make despair worse.

You are not the first believer to want to die. Elijah sat under a tree and prayed, "I have had enough, Yahweh... Take my life" (1 Kings 19:4). God did not scold him. He let him sleep, fed him, and sent him back to the work with companions (1 Kings 19:5-8). Paul once "despaired even of life" (2 Corinthians 1:8).

God is "near to the brokenhearted" (Psalm 34:18). There is nowhere you can go that He is not already there (Psalm 139:7-10). Nothing, "neither death nor life... nor anything else in all creation," can separate His people "from the love of God that is in Christ Jesus our Lord" (Romans 8:38-39).

The way you feel now is not the way you will always feel. Hold on, reach out, and let others hold on to you.
