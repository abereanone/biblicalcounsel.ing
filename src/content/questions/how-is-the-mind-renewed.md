The mind is renewed as we fill it with God's truth, take our thoughts captive to Christ, and set our minds on things above. Over time, God's way of seeing things becomes our own.

## Proofs
- Romans 12:2
- 2 Corinthians 10:5
- Philippians 4:8
- Colossians 3:2
- Psalm 1:2

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

"Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind" (Romans 12:2). Much of our trouble comes from thinking that is shaped by fear, by the world or by our own desires, rather than by God's truth.

Scripture gives practical ways to renew the mind:

- **Fill it.** The blessed man delights in God's law and "on His law he meditates day and night" (Psalm 1:2). Read, memorize and think about Scripture, especially passages that speak to your struggle.
- **Examine it.** "We take captive every thought to make it obedient to Christ" (2 Corinthians 10:5). Notice what you are telling yourself, and ask whether it is true according to God.
- **Redirect it.** "Whatever is true, whatever is honorable, whatever is right, whatever is pure... think on these things" (Philippians 4:8).
- **Lift it.** "Set your minds on things above, not on earthly things" (Colossians 3:2).

A simple practice: when an anxious, bitter or tempting thought comes, write it down, find a verse that answers it, and pray that verse back to God. Repeated over weeks, this reshapes how you think.
