The Association of Certified Biblical Counselors certifies counselors who complete theological and counseling training, pass written exams, are evaluated by their church, and counsel under supervision, all on the conviction that Scripture is sufficient for counseling. It is one widely recognized mark of training in biblical counseling.

## Proofs
- 2 Timothy 2:2
- 2 Timothy 2:15
- 1 Timothy 4:16
- Proverbs 27:17

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

ACBC was founded in 1976 as the National Association of Nouthetic Counselors, growing out of Jay Adams' work, to train and certify counselors committed to counseling from the Word of God.

As currently described on its website, certification has three phases:

1. **Learning**: a fundamentals course, observation of real counseling, and substantial reading in biblical counseling and theology.
2. **Exams and evaluations**: written theology and counseling exams, and evaluations from the candidate's pastor and others who know him.
3. **Supervision**: many hours of counseling under the supervision of an experienced ACBC Fellow.

Certified counselors affirm ACBC's Standards of Doctrine and Standards of Conduct, renew annually with their pastor's endorsement, and continue their education.

A few observations:

- **Training is good.** "Make every effort to present yourself approved to God, an unashamed workman who accurately handles the word of truth" (2 Timothy 2:15). Supervised practice sharpens wisdom: "As iron sharpens iron, so one man sharpens another" (Proverbs 27:17).
- **Certification is not required to counsel.** Every believer is called to counsel others with the Word.
- **Check details with ACBC.** Requirements change; see biblicalcounseling.com for current information.

This site is independent of ACBC and grateful for its work.
