Yes. God has given us in His Word everything we need to understand the soul, its problems and its cure, and to live a life that pleases Him. Scripture does not tell us everything about everything, but it tells us everything we need for life and godliness.

## Proofs
- 2 Peter 1:3
- 2 Timothy 3:16-17
- Psalm 19:7-11
- Hebrews 4:12

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

"His divine power has given us everything we need for life and godliness through the knowledge of Him who called us" (2 Peter 1:3). Scripture makes "the man of God... complete, fully equipped for every good work" (2 Timothy 3:17).

The sufficiency of Scripture does **not** mean:

- that the Bible is a medical textbook, or that Christians should ignore doctors;
- that we cannot learn true facts from observing people and the world;
- that a counselor never needs wisdom about circumstances, schedules or practical matters.

It **does** mean:

- God's Word gives the true account of what people are (made in His image, fallen, redeemable), what is wrong with them (sin and its misery in a fallen world) and how they change (by grace in Christ);
- no outside theory needs to supply what Scripture lacks on these matters;
- any idea about the soul must be tested by Scripture: "To the law and to the testimony! If they do not speak according to this word, they have no light of dawn" (Isaiah 8:20).

The Word "judges the thoughts and intentions of the heart" (Hebrews 4:12). No other counsel can reach that deep.
