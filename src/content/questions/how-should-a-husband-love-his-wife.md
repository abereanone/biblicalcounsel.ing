As Christ loved the church: sacrificially, patiently and tenderly, putting her good before his comfort, leading by serving, and never being harsh with her.

## Proofs
- Ephesians 5:25-29
- Colossians 3:19
- 1 Peter 3:7
- 1 Corinthians 13:4-7

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

"Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave Himself up for her" (Ephesians 5:25). That is the highest standard any husband could be given. Christ's love for the church cost Him everything.

What it looks like:

- **Sacrifice.** Putting her needs before your preferences, your time and your comfort.
- **Nourishing and cherishing.** "Husbands ought to love their wives as their own bodies... he nourishes and cherishes it, just as Christ does the church" (Ephesians 5:28-29). Care for her spiritually, emotionally and physically.
- **Understanding and honor.** "Treat your wives with consideration... and with honor as fellow heirs of the gracious gift of life, so that your prayers will not be hindered" (1 Peter 3:7). God takes it so seriously that a husband's mistreatment of his wife affects his prayers.
- **Gentleness.** "Husbands, love your wives and do not be harsh with them" (Colossians 3:19).
- **Spiritual leadership.** Leading in prayer, the Word and the church, as a servant, not a tyrant.

Headship in Scripture is never a license for control. It is a call to take the lead in laying down your life.
