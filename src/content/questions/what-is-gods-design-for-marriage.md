Marriage is a lifelong covenant union between one man and one woman, made by God, in which they become one flesh. It pictures Christ and the church, and it is the place God designed for companionship, sexual intimacy and the raising of children.

## Proofs
- Genesis 2:18-24
- Matthew 19:4-6
- Ephesians 5:31-32
- Hebrews 13:4

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

God created marriage before sin entered the world: "For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be united to his wife, and they will become one flesh" (Genesis 2:24). Jesus affirmed it: "So they are no longer two, but one flesh. Therefore what God has joined together, let man not separate" (Matthew 19:6).

Several things follow:

- **It is a covenant**, not merely a contract or a feeling. Its vows are made before God.
- **It is lifelong.** It ends at death, not when love fades.
- **It is one man and one woman**, as God made them "male and female" (Matthew 19:4).
- **It is exclusive.** "Marriage should be honored by all and the marriage bed kept undefiled" (Hebrews 13:4).
- **It has a purpose beyond itself.** Paul says marriage is a "mystery" that speaks of "Christ and the church" (Ephesians 5:32). A husband's sacrificial love and a wife's respectful trust display the gospel.

Understanding this design gives hope in counseling. Marriage is hard because two sinners live closely together. But God, who made marriage, also gives the grace to keep its vows.
