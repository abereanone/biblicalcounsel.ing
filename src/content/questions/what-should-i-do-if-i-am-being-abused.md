Get safe. If you are in danger, call 911. Abuse is a serious sin and often a crime, and God does not require you to stay in harm's way. Tell your pastor or another trusted person, and let the civil authorities do their God-given work of protecting you.

## Proofs
- Proverbs 27:12
- Romans 13:3-4
- Psalm 11:5
- Acts 5:29
- Psalm 9:9

**If you are in danger now, call 911.** In the United States you can also call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788. If a child is being harmed, report it to the police or to child protective services right away.

Abuse is when someone uses power to control, harm, frighten or degrade another: physical violence, sexual abuse, threats, intimidation, isolation, or controlling money and movement. God sees it: "His soul hates the lover of violence" (Psalm 11:5).

What Scripture says to you:

- **It is right to flee danger.** "The prudent see danger and take cover" (Proverbs 27:12). David fled from Saul; Paul was let down over a wall to escape (Acts 9:25). Leaving to be safe is not sin.
- **Report crimes.** God gave civil rulers to be "an agent of retribution to the wrongdoer" (Romans 13:4). Reporting abuse to the police is not unforgiving or unloving; it is how God protects the vulnerable.
- **Submission never means submitting to sin.** "We must obey God rather than men" (Acts 5:29). No husband, parent or church leader has authority from God to harm you.
- **You are not to blame** for someone else's sin against you.

For church leaders: believe and protect first, involve the authorities, never pressure a victim to reconcile with an abuser who has not been proven repentant, and do not offer joint marriage counseling while abuse is ongoing.

The Lord is "a refuge for the oppressed, a stronghold in times of trouble" (Psalm 9:9).
