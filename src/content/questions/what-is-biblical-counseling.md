Biblical counseling is one Christian helping another to understand his life and problems the way God's Word does, and to respond to them in faith and obedience to Christ, by the power of the Holy Spirit.

## Proofs
- Colossians 1:28
- Romans 15:14
- 2 Timothy 3:16-17
- Galatians 6:1-2

Biblical counseling is the ministry of the Word in personal conversation. A pastor preaches the Word to a congregation; a counselor applies the same Word to one person's particular troubles: a marriage in conflict, a mind full of fear, a heart heavy with grief or guilt.

Several things make it *biblical*:

- **Its authority.** Scripture, not a theory of personality, defines what the problem is and what the answer is. "All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for instruction, for conviction, for correction, and for training in righteousness" (2 Timothy 3:16).
- **Its center.** Christ is the answer, not merely a helpful addition. "We proclaim Him, admonishing and teaching everyone with all wisdom, so that we may present everyone perfect in Christ" (Colossians 1:28).
- **Its power.** Lasting change comes by the Holy Spirit working through the Word, not by technique.
- **Its setting.** It belongs in the life of the church, where believers are "able to instruct one another" (Romans 15:14).

Biblical counseling is not cold or merely corrective. It listens carefully, weeps with those who weep, and offers real hope, because the God of the Bible really does help.
