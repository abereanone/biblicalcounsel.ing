The Holy Spirit gives the new heart, opens our eyes to the Word, convicts us of sin, produces His fruit in us, and gives the power to obey. Counseling that forgets the Spirit becomes mere self-improvement.

## Proofs
- Ezekiel 36:26-27
- Galatians 5:16
- Galatians 5:22-23
- John 16:13
- Romans 8:13

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

God promised, "I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit within you... And I will put My Spirit within you and cause you to walk in My statutes" (Ezekiel 36:26-27). Every true change in a believer is the Spirit's work.

The Spirit:

- **regenerates**, giving spiritual life to the dead;
- **illuminates**, guiding us into truth through the Word He inspired (John 16:13);
- **convicts**, showing us our sin so that we turn from it;
- **empowers**: "by the Spirit you put to death the deeds of the body" (Romans 8:13);
- **produces fruit**: "love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control" (Galatians 5:22-23).

This shapes counseling in two ways. It humbles the counselor, who cannot change anyone's heart. And it gives hope to the counselee, who may feel powerless: "walk by the Spirit, and you will not gratify the desires of the flesh" (Galatians 5:16). The power to change does not come from inside you. It comes from God, who lives in you.
