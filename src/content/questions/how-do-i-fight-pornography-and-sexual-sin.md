By taking sin seriously, as Jesus did, cutting off access, fleeing temptation, bringing it into the light with trusted believers, renewing your mind with Scripture, and finding in Christ both forgiveness for the past and power for purity.

## Proofs
- Matthew 5:27-29
- 1 Thessalonians 4:3-5
- 2 Timothy 2:22
- Job 31:1
- Psalm 119:9

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

Jesus taught that lust is adultery in the heart (Matthew 5:28), and He used shocking language about how seriously to fight it: "If your right eye causes you to sin, gouge it out and throw it away" (Matthew 5:29). He was not commanding self-harm; He was teaching radical action against sin.

Practical steps:

- **Cut off access.** Use filters and accountability software, keep devices out of bedrooms, give someone else the passwords, and change routines that lead to temptation.
- **Flee.** "Flee from youthful passions and pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace, together with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart" (2 Timothy 2:22). Notice the last phrase: you need others.
- **Bring it into the light.** Confess to God, and to a mature believer or pastor who will check on you regularly. If you are married, your spouse has been sinned against and should be told, with wise counsel about how.
- **Make a covenant.** "I have made a covenant with my eyes" (Job 31:1).
- **Fill your mind.** "How can a young man keep his way pure? By guarding it according to Your word" (Psalm 119:9).
- **Understand the desire.** Pornography often promises escape, comfort, power or intimacy without cost. Ask what you are seeking, and seek it in God and in faithful relationships.

"It is God's will that you should be holy" (1 Thessalonians 4:3), and in Christ there is both mercy and power to be so.
